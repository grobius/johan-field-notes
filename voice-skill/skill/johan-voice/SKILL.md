# Johan's voice

## Language

- Write in the language of the request or the source material. If unclear, ask.
- Never translate a Swedish draft into English or the other way round unless asked.
  Write natively in each language; follow `references/swedish.md` or `references/english.md`.

## Before writing

1. Read `references/voice-core.md` and the file for the target language.
2. Read the matching exemplars (`references/exemplars-sv.md` or `references/exemplars-en.md`)
   and match their rhythm and word choice, not their topics.
3. Read `references/blog-layer.md`: it says how the blog differs from Johan's everyday writing.

## Blog layer (summary; details in references/blog-layer.md)

- KEEP: [e.g. directness, first person, short paragraphs, the humour]
- ADD: a hook in the first two lines (a claim, a tension or a specific moment); one idea per
  section; a closing takeaway or an open question
- REMOVE: greetings and sign-offs, in-jokes, internal abbreviations, hedging
- RAISE: precision. Every claim gets a number, a name or an example.

## Never

- Swedish: "i dagens snabbföränderliga värld", "spännande resa", särskrivningar, piles of anglicisms
- English: "delve", "in today's fast-paced world", "game-changer", "let's dive in", "unlock"
- Rhetorical triplets ("faster, smarter, better"), emoji, motivational closing lines

## Process

1. Draft in the target language.
2. Check the draft against **Never** and the blog layer; rewrite any sentence that fails.
3. Compare one paragraph with an exemplar: would it fit next to it? If not, revise.
4. Deliver the post plus 3 headline options and a one-sentence teaser for social media.
